01 · About

A bit about me

I'm an AI and Machine Learning Engineer from Peshawar, Pakistan, currently finishing my BS in Artificial Intelligence (expected 2027). Most of my time goes into building and shipping machine learning systems in Python, PyTorch, and TensorFlow — not just training models, but getting them into people's hands.

I've worked across computer vision (CNN and Vision Transformer models), NLP, and LLM-based agent systems. My favorite build so far is a research-automation agent on the Model Context Protocol (MCP) that connects LangChain to Groq-hosted LLMs — it turned a tedious literature-search workflow into a single conversation.

I'm comfortable owning the whole pipeline: data preprocessing, training and evaluation, then deployment as a Flask or FastAPI app. Right now I'm looking for an AI/ML or GenAI Engineering role where I can do that in a real production setting, alongside people I can learn from.
